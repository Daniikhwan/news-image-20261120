# Friday 20 November 2026 — The World in One Day

An entry for make.fun `news-image-20261120`: one image summing up the
world news of Friday 20 November 2026.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) draws the
infographic and writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
(default `./out/entry.png`). The output is deterministic: two runs write
byte-identical PNG files.

## The six stories

1. **GTA VI passes $2.36 billion** — 24.6m copies in under 24 hours,
   the biggest entertainment launch ever (Sensor Tower / Deadline).
2. **Cyclone Akvan hits India** — landfall near Gopalpur at noon, winds
   90–100 kmph, around 50,000 people evacuated (IMD).
3. **COP31 closing day in Antalya** — the fossil-free draft faces its
   final test, the final text due by midnight (Reuters / UN).
4. **Trump, Putin issue peace statement** — joint text after the Shenzhen
   summit, omitting Ukraine (Reuters).
5. **Nicholls 113 puts NZ on top** — New Zealand 243 for 5 on day 2 of
   the Wellington Test against India.
6. **Gulf refinery restarts** — Bahrain's Bapco nears full output after
   strikes on Gulf energy sites.
